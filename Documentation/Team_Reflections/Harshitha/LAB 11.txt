1. In this lab, I learned how RAM and Flash memory behave differently when the ESP32 is restarted.
2. The RAM counter remained at 1 in all three runs because RAM does not retain its values after a reset.
3. The Flash counter increased from 2 to 3 and 4 because Preferences stores data in non-volatile memory.
4. RAM writes took around 0–1 microseconds, while Flash writes took approximately 703–704 microseconds.
5. RAM reads were also faster, taking only 1–2 microseconds compared to 131–132 microseconds for Flash reads.
6. This experiment showed that RAM is suitable for temporary data, while Flash is useful for information that must survive resets.
7. Frequent Flash writes should be avoided since they take much longer than RAM writes and wear out Flash endurance over time.
8. I found it interesting that the Flash counter continued from its previous value even after restarting the board.
