1. In this lab, I understood the difference between volatile RAM and non-volatile Flash memory by testing what happens after resetting the ESP32.
2. The RAM counter remained at 1 in all three runs because RAM loses its stored values during a reset and the global variable is initialized again.
3. In contrast, the Flash counter increased from 2 to 3 and then 4 because Preferences stores data permanently in the Flash memory.
4. I observed that RAM write operations took only 0–1 microseconds, while Flash writes required approximately 703–704 microseconds.
5. RAM reads took around 1–2 microseconds, whereas Flash reads took 131–132 microseconds.
6. This showed me that internal RAM is much faster than Flash because Flash storage involves additional operations to access and update persistent data.
7. Writing to Flash in a tight loop is bad practice because each write takes around 703–704 us and repeated writes wear down finite Flash endurance.
8. I was surprised that the Flash counter retained its value even after multiple resets while the RAM counter started from 1 every time.
